### Ingredients

- Soondubu
- Enoki mushrooms
- Sliced pork belly
- 1/2 Onion
- 4 Small green onions
- 2 Eggs
- 1/3 Zucchini
- Sliced burdock root 1 scoop
- 2 Cheongyang chili peppers
- Water 375 ml
- Mirin 2 tbsp
- White pepper
- Korean red chili powder 2 tbsp
- Soy sauce 1 tbsp
- Oyster sauce 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic 1 tbsp
- Worcestershire sauce 1 tbsp
