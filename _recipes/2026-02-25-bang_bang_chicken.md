### Ingredients

- Chicken Thigh (Boneless, Skinless), Diced 900g
- Onion powder 8 g
- Garlic powder 8 g
- Smoked paprika 8 g
- Black pepper

### Bang Bang Marinade

- Mayonnaise 90g
- Yogurt 100g
- Sweet chilli sauce 125 ml
- Sriracha 45g
- Lime juice 1 tbsp
- Black Pepper

### Personal Notes
Need to use the oven instead of air fryer.
