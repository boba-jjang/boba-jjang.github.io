### Ingredients

- Light brown rice
- Olive oil
- Cabbage
- Braised pork belly
- Zucchini
- Parsley
- Cilantro
- Drops of Rayu Japanese Chili Oil
- Black pepper

### Personal Notes
Rendering the fat from the braised pork belly to use as an oil would enhance the overall flavor profile. In the next version, I should also reduce the amount of parsley and cilantro to prevent the herbs from overpowering the flavor.
