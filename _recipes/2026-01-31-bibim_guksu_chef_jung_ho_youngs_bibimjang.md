### Sauce Ingredients

- Coarse red chili pepper powder 3 tbsp
- Sugar 2 tbsp
- Vinegar 2 tbsp
- Soy sauce 2 tbsp
- Worcestershire sauce 1/2 tbsp
- Black pepper 1/2 tbsp
- Minced garlic 1 tbsp
- Chilsung Cider 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil 2 tbsp

### Toppings (Up to Your Preference)

- Onion
- Cucumber
- Lettuce
- 1 Egg

### Personal Notes

- Put Jikabiyaki on top.
