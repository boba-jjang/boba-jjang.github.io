### Ingredients

- Thinly sliced pork 350g
- 1 Onion
- 1 Red potato
- 1/2 Carrot
- Light brown rice
- 5/8 box Japanese curry block
- Water 900 ml
- Sugar 2 tbsp
- Water 2 tbsp (for caramelizing onion)
- Minced garlic 1/2 tsp
- Worcestershire sauce 1/2 tbsp
- Grounded coffee 1 tsp
- Butter 1 tbsp
