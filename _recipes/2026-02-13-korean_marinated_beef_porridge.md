### Ingredients

- White rice porridge (made while seasoning Donabe) 650 ml
- Korean marinated beef
- Korean marinade sauce 1 tbsp
- Salt
- Avocado oil
- 1 Green onion
- 1/2 Onion
- 1/4 Carrot
- Drops of Rayu Japanese Chili Oil

### Personal Notes
The beef could be cut into smaller pieces and cooked slightly longer for better texture.
