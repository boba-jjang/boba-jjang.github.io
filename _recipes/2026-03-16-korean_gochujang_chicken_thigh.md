### Ingredients

- Chicken thigh
- Gochujang 70g
- Allulose 35g
- Cooking oil
- Garlic powder 2 tsp
- Onion powder 1 tsp
- Smoked paprika 1 tsp
- White pepper 1 tsp
- Chinese five spice 1 tsp
- Salt
