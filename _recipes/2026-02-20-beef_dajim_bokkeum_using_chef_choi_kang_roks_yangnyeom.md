### Marinade Ingredients

- Soy sauce 300 ml
- Water 60 ml
- Dark brown sugar 100g
- Honey 50g
- Cooking sake 30 ml
- Yuzu kosho 5g (adjust to your preference)
- Sesame oil 50 ml
- Canned pineapple 80g
- Apple 1/2
- Onion 1/4
- Green onion 1/2 stalk
- Garlic cloves 3
- Ginger 10g

### Additional Ingredients

- Marinated beef
- 1 Big green onion
- Garlic 3 cloves
- Sesame seeds
- Oil
- 1 Onion

### Personal Notes
Reduce the amount of Yuzu Kosho back to 2g.
