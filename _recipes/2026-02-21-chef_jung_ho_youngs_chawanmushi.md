### Ingredients

- 2 Eggs
- Ichiban dashi 150 ml
- Mirin 1 tbsp
- Salt
- Soy sauce 1/3 tbsp

### Personal Notes
Texture was good. To get rid of the oil residues on the bottom and get that mirror-like surface, I need to filter the egg mixture after combining it with the dashi. Make sure to pour slowly through a sieve to avoid creating unnecessary bubbles.
