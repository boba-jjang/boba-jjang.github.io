### Ingredients

- Beef 300g
- 1 Green onion
- Dried seaweed
- 1 Egg
- Brown rice cake 800g
- Fish sauce 2 tbsp
- Sesame oil 3 tbsp (used to cook beef first)

### Meat Marinade Ingredients

- Soup soy sauce 2 tbsp
- Potato starch 2 tbsp
- Minced garlic 1 tbsp
- Black pepper
