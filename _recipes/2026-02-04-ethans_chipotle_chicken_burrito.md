### Marinade Ingredients

- 2 Garlic cloves
- 1/4 Red onion
- Juice from 1/2 orange
- Juice from 1/2 lime
- 1 Habanero
- Achiote paste 30g

### Inside Burrito

- Shredded lettuce
- Some jalapeno
- Chopped tomatoes
- Spread guacamole
- Grated cheese
- Salsa

### Personal Notes
Cooking chicken with achiote paste brings out a lot of flavor, but in this case it was a bit too much. The saltiness can be adjusted, right now the flavor intensity outweighs the salt. It might be worth trying a different recipe that uses achiote paste more subtly.
