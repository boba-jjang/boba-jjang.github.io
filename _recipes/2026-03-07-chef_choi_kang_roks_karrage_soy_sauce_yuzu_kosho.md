### Soy Sauce Coating Ingredients

- Chicken thigh
- Grated onion 15 g
- Minced ginger 1/2 tbsp
- Minced garlic 1 tbsp
- Cooking sake 1/2 tbsp
- Soy Sauce 1/2 tbsp
- Oil 1 tbsp
- Mirin 1/2 tbsp
- Egg 1 tbsp (beaten)

### Coating ingredients

- All-purpose flour 4 tbsp
- Cornstarch 2 tbsp
(2:1 ratio, total 6 tbsp)

### Yuzu Kosho Coating Ingredients

- Chicken thigh
- Grated onion 30 g
- Cooking sake 1 tbsp
- Yuzu kosho 1 1/2 tbsp
- Sesame oil 2 tsp
- Egg 1 1/2 tbsp (beaten)

### Coating ingredients

- All-purpose flour 4 tbsp
- Cornstarch 2 tbsp
(2:1 ratio, total 6 tbsp)

### Personal Notes
I think I need to make the coating a bit saltier; the flavor intensity drops by about 20–25% after deep frying, even though it tastes fine beforehand.
