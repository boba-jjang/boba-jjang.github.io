### Ingredients

- Chicken wings
- Achiote paste 1 tbsp
- Garlic powder 0.5 tbsp
- Onion powder 0.5 tbsp
- Cilantro 0.5 tbsp
- Oregano 0.5 tbsp
- Cumin 0.5 tbsp
- Peri peri powder 0.5 tbsp
- Ground coriander 0.5 tbsp

### Personal Notes
Make sure that wings are dried, no oil.
