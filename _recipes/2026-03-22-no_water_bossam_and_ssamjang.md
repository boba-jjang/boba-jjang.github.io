### Bossam Ingredients

- Pork shoulders
- 1 Onion
- 1 Big green onion
- 1 Apple
- 3 basil leaves
- Cooking sake 2 tbsp
- Sea salt
- 7 Garlics
- 3 Ginger slices

### Ssamjang ingredients

- Soy bean paste 3 tbsp
- Red chili pepper paste 1 tbsp
- Chilsung cider 3 tbsp
- Almonds
- Sesame oil 0.5 tbsp
- Minced garlic 0.5 tbsp
