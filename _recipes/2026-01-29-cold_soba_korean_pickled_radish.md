### Soba Ingredients

- Tsuyu 50 ml
- Water 350 ml (1:7, adjust to tsuyu saltiness)
- Dried seaweed
- Wasabi
- Grated daikon radish

### Korean Pickled Radish Ingredients

- Korean radish 3400g
- Red pepper powder 120g
- Minced garlic 200g
- Minced ginger 40g
- Korean "new sugar" 10g
- Sea salt (coarse) 50g
- Green onions
