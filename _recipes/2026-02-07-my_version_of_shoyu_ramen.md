### Ingredients

- Niiban Dashi 450 ml
- Water 100 ml
- 1 Green onion / oil
- Shiitake mushroom
- Wood ear mushroom
- 1 Onion
- Gai Choy
- Drops of Rayu Japanese Chili Oil

### Shoyu Tare Ingredients

- Soy sauce 3 tbsp
- Mirin 1 tbsp
- Cooking sake 1 tbsp
- Allulose 1 tsp
