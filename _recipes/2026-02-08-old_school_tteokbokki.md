### Ingredients

- Flour rice cake 450g
- Water 550 ml
- Fish cakes 3 slices
- 1 Big green onion
- 1 Egg (boiled)
- Soy Sauce 2 tbsp
- Sugar 2 tbsp
- Allulose 3 tbsp
- Beef dashida 1 tsp
- Shredded kale
- Grated cheese
- Red pepper powder 2 tbsp
- Konjac yam noodles

### Personal Notes
This recipe's taste is more on the sweeter side (like old school street food-style taste). Try to grind the coarse-grained red pepper powder to make it finer grained.
