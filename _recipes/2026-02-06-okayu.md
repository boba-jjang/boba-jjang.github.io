### Ingredients

- White rice porridge (made while seasoning Donabe) 350 ml
- Ichiban Dashi 250 ml
- 1 Green onion
- 2 Eggs (stirred)
- Mirin 1 tsp
- Cooking sake 1 tsp
- Drops of Rayu Japanese Chili Oil
