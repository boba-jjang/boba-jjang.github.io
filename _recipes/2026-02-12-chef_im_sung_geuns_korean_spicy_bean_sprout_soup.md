### Ingredients

- Anchovy broth 6 cup
- Bean sprout 340g
- Egg
- Big green onion 1 stalk
- 2 Cheongyang chili pepper
- Chili pepper powder 2 tbsp
- Salted shrimp 1.25 tbsp
- Salt
- Minced garlic 1 tbsp

### Personal Notes
Omitting the fresh chili peppers had a bigger impact on the flavor than I expected. I should include them next time. The salted shrimp I used also wasn't very fresh, which likely affected the flavor.
